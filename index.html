About and Privacy

Mind Archive is for private long-term writing: thoughts, feelings, notes, collections, and personal timelines.

Your posts are encrypted in your browser before they are stored on the server. The server stores encrypted vault data, not readable journal text.

Your password is used in the browser to unlock your archive. The raw password is not sent to the server.

Password recovery only works with the recovery key shown during sign-up. The server cannot recover an archive without it.

Backups matter. Use the Markdown and JSON export tools regularly if the archive is important to you.

Save this recovery key now.

It can reset your password if you forget it. It will not be shown again.

Each archive is encrypted in your browser before it is stored on the server. Other users cannot open it, and the server cannot read it without your password or your recovery key.
